# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

GTA6 Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel "Wasted Daily | GTA 6" on their own computer. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

## YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What the app accesses

When the channel owner connects their Google account, the app asks for two permissions:

- **youtube.upload**: to upload video clips that the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own channel.
- **youtube.readonly**: to read the connected channel's name, picture, subscriber count and the status of videos it uploaded, to show them in the app.

The app does not read, collect or store any other Google account data. It does not access other people's accounts, comments or personal information.

## How information is stored

- The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file only that computer user can read.
- Video files, titles, descriptions and the posting schedule are stored only on the owner's computer.
- No information is sent to any server other than Google's and YouTube's own services, which are needed to upload the videos.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, share, rent or transfer any user data to anyone. No data is used for advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Disconnecting YouTube in the app deletes the stored token and revokes it with Google. Access can also be removed at any time from the [Google security settings page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
